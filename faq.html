FAQ

Questions about vape recycling.

Can’t find your answer? Call 0460 555 222 or send us a message.

The basics

Why can’t I put a vape in my bin?

Vapes contain a lithium-ion battery that is usually still partly charged. When bins are compacted in the truck or at a facility, the battery can be crushed or punctured and catch fire. That applies to rubbish, recycling and green bins, and to public litter bins.

My vape is empty. Is it still dangerous?

Usually, yes. A vape can run out of e-liquid long before its battery is flat, so most “empty” disposables still hold charge.

Should I take the battery out first?

Only if it’s designed to come out easily, as in many rechargeable vapes. Tape both ends and bring it with the vape. Don’t cut open or pull apart disposable vapes. That’s how batteries get damaged and catch fire.

What do you accept?

Disposable and rechargeable vapes, pod systems and mods, pods, cartridges, tanks and coils, removed vape batteries with taped ends, and vape chargers. See the full list.

What happens to the vapes you collect?

They’re kept separate from general waste and handled as battery waste. Batteries are separated from the casings, and the metals and plastics are split so each material can go to a recycler that can use it.

Collection bins

Who can get a collection bin?

Any organisation that collects or ends up with vapes: pharmacies, schools and universities, councils, workplaces, venues and events, health services, property managers and retail centres. Request a bin and we’ll confirm whether we service your area.

How much does a bin cost?

It depends on how many bins you need, how many vapes you expect and how often they need collecting. We confirm the price with you before anything is delivered, and there’s no commitment from sending a request.

How often do you collect?

Whatever suits the site. Most choose on call, where you contact us when the bin is close to full, or a regular schedule. We collect the full bin and leave an empty one.

Where should the bin go?

Somewhere indoors or covered, out of direct sun and away from heaters and anything flammable. In schools, keep it in a staff area rather than somewhere students can reach. We’ll help you pick the spot when we deliver.

What if something other than vapes ends up in the bin?

The signage makes it clear what goes in, which keeps contamination low. If you notice sharps, liquids or a damaged vape in the bin, don’t reach in. Call us and we’ll advise.

Do you service more than one site?

Yes. Tell us how many locations you have on the request form and list them in the notes, and we’ll put together a plan for all of them.

Drop-off

Where can I drop off vapes?

At Recycle North Geelong, 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong VIC 3215. It’s open six days a week and undercover. Let staff know you have vapes when you arrive. Drop-off details.

Do I need to book?

Not for a few vapes from home. For bulk lots, such as confiscated vapes or stored stock, please book a bulk drop-off first so we can receive them safely.

Is there a charge to drop off?

Please call 0460 555 222 to check current drop-off charges. For bulk lots, we confirm any cost when we reply to your booking.

How should I bring them in?

In a sturdy container with a lid, such as a tin, glass jar or plastic tub. Keep damaged, swollen or leaking vapes separate and tell staff. Don’t leave vapes loose in a hot car.

Schools

We confiscate vapes. What should we do with them?

Store them whole in a lidded metal or glass container in the office, away from heat and out of students’ reach. Once you have a collection bin, they go straight in. For an existing backlog, book a bulk drop-off or ask us about a one-off collection.
