For workplaces & venues

Keep vapes out of your bins and your bin room.

Vapes get used in smoking areas, left on bar tops, handed into lost property and dropped into general waste. Each one is a lithium battery heading for your skip or bin room. A dedicated bin keeps them separate.

Why it matters on site

Your waste contractor can’t see a vape in a bag.

Commercial bins are compacted just like household ones. A vape tossed into a general waste bag goes into a compactor truck and on to a facility, where a crushed battery can start a fire.

How it works

Nothing extra for your team to manage.

  1. Request

    Tell us your sites and roughly how many vapes turn up.

  2. We deliver

    A lidded bin and signage, placed somewhere covered and away from heat.

  3. People use it

    Staff, patrons or contractors drop vapes in. No handling required.

  4. We collect & swap

    On call or on a schedule, and the vapes are recycled properly.

One bin. One less fire risk.
