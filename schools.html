For schools & universities

A drawer of confiscated vapes is a drawer of charged batteries.

Every vape taken off a student still has a lithium battery inside. Kept loose in an office drawer or tipped into the general bin, they’re a fire risk. A collection bin gives them somewhere safe to go, and we take them away.

Request a school bin Clear a backlog

What usually happens

  • Confiscated vapes pile up in a drawer or envelope in the office
  • Loose batteries sit touching keys, coins and each other
  • Eventually they go in the general bin, and into a compactor truck
  • Nobody is sure who is responsible for them

With a collection bin

  • Confiscated vapes go straight into one lidded bin in a staff area
  • Stored cool and covered, away from students
  • We collect and swap it when it’s full
  • One clear process for every staff member
How it works

A simple process for staff.

Built for a front office, a year-level coordinator or a campus security team.

  1. Request

    Tell us your school or campus, number of sites and rough volumes.

  2. We deliver

    A lidded bin for a staff-only area, with signage for staff.

  3. Staff drop vapes in

    Whole, never opened. Swollen or leaking ones are kept apart and we’re called.

  4. We collect & swap

    On call or each term, and the vapes are recycled through our facility.

School questions

We already have a pile of confiscated vapes. What now?

Put them in a lidded metal tin or glass jar in a cool place, and book a bulk drop-off or ask about a one-off collection when you request a bin.

Where should the bin go?

In a staff-only area such as the front office or a coordinator’s office, out of direct sun and away from heaters. Not in a corridor or anywhere students can reach.

Can we cover several campuses?

Yes. List each campus in the notes on the request form and we’ll plan collections across all of them.

Do we need to record what goes in?

That’s up to your school’s own policy. The bin simply gives confiscated vapes a safe, final destination.
