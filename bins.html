For organisations

A vape bin that we supply, collect and swap.

Give customers, students, staff or the public one safe place for vapes. We deliver the bin and signage, collect it when it’s full, and take care of what happens next.

Request a bin 0460 555 222
What you get

Everything needed to run a collection point.

You provide the spot. We provide the bin, the signage and the collections.

A lidded collection bin

Sized to how many vapes you expect, for a counter, a back office or a public area. We’ll recommend the right one.

Clear signage

So people know what goes in (vapes, pods, cartridges) and what doesn’t (loose e-liquid, sharps, cigarette butts, rubbish).

Collection and swap

Call us when it’s full or set a regular schedule. We take the full bin away and leave a clean, empty one.

How it works

From request to recycled in five steps.

  1. Request

    Fill in the form below. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Confirm

    We contact you to confirm bin size, location, service area and cost.

  3. Deliver

    We drop off the bin and signage and show you where it works best.

  4. Collect

    Full bins are collected and swapped, on call or on a schedule.

  5. Recycle

    Vapes are made safe and separated so batteries, metals and plastics can be recovered.

Good places for a bin

Who uses a vape collection bin.

Pharmacies

A return point at the counter for customers and quit programs.

Schools & universities

For confiscated or surrendered vapes. Kept in the office, away from students.

Councils

Resident drop-off at service centres, libraries and transfer stations.

Workplaces & sites

Break rooms, smoking areas, depots and building sites.

Venues & events

Lost-and-found vapes, bar areas and event amnesty points.

Health services

Hospitals, clinics and community health quit programs.

Property & strata

Shared bin rooms in apartment buildings and managed sites.

Retail centres

Centre management, customer service desks and staff areas.

Safe storage tips
Looking after the bin

Four rules for a safe collection point.

Out of the sun

Away from heaters, windows and anything that gets hot.

Lid closed

Keep the lid on between drop-ins.

Vapes only

No loose e-liquid, sharps, butts or general rubbish.

Damaged? Isolate it

Swollen, leaking or hot vapes go in a separate metal container. Call us.
