Before you come

Three things to do at home.

These take a minute and make the trip safe for you and everyone who handles the vapes after you.

Tape loose batteries

If a rechargeable vape’s battery comes out easily, take it out and put tape over both ends. Don’t pull apart disposables.

Use a sturdy container

A tin, glass jar or plastic tub with a lid. Not a paper bag, and not loose in the car.

Tell staff on arrival

Let the team know you have vapes so they go to the right place, not the general waste area.

{{i:warn}}

Swollen, leaking or hot vapes

Keep them separate from the rest, in a metal tin or a jar of sand, away from anything flammable. Tell staff before you hand them over. If one is smoking or on fire, move away and call 000.
