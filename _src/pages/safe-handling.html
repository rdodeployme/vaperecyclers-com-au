Safe handling

How to handle used vapes safely.

Whether you have one vape or a box of them, the rules are the same: keep them whole, keep them cool, and keep them out of every household bin.

The basics

Do this, not that.

Do Safe

  • {{i:check}}
    Keep used vapes in a lidded tin, jar or tubSomething that won’t burn or tear if a battery heats up.
  • {{i:check}}
    Store them somewhere cool and dryAway from sun, heaters, car dashboards and anything flammable.
  • {{i:check}}
    Tape the ends of removable batteriesOnly if the battery comes out easily, as in many rechargeable vapes.
  • {{i:check}}
    Keep them away from children and petsE-liquid can contain nicotine, which is poisonous if swallowed.
  • {{i:check}}
    Bring them to a drop-off point or collection bin

Don’t Unsafe

  • {{i:x}}
    Put vapes in your rubbish, recycling or green binIncluding public litter bins. They can start fires in trucks and facilities.
  • {{i:x}}
    Pull apart disposable vapesCutting into the battery can make it spark or catch fire.
  • {{i:x}}
    Crush, puncture or squash them to save space
  • {{i:x}}
    Store them loose in a drawer, bag or gloveboxBatteries touching metal or each other can short.
  • {{i:x}}
    Pour e-liquid down the sink or onto the ground
Damaged vapes

If a vape is swollen, leaking or hot.

A swollen or hot battery is a warning sign. Treat it carefully and keep it apart from other vapes.

  1. Move it away from anything that burns

    Place it on a non-flammable surface such as concrete or tiles, outdoors if you can.

  2. Isolate it

    Once it has cooled, put it in a metal tin or a jar of dry sand on its own. Don’t seal it airtight while it’s warm.

  3. Tell us before you bring it

    Call 0460 555 222 or mention it on your booking so we can receive it separately.

{{i:flame}}

Smoke, flames or a hissing battery?

Don’t touch it. Move people away, and call 000. Lithium battery fires can flare up again after they appear to go out.

What happens next

Where your vapes go.

Collected vapes are never put into general waste. They’re kept separate, handled as battery waste and broken down so the materials can be recovered.

  1. Collected

    From collection bins and our drop-off area, kept apart from other waste.

  2. Made safe

    Stored and transported as battery waste, with damaged units handled separately.

  3. Separated

    Batteries are removed from casings, and metals and plastics are split into streams.

  4. Recovered

    Each stream goes to recyclers who can turn it back into usable material.
