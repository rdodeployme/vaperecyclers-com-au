Why it matters

A small device with three big problems.

A “dead” vape usually isn’t. The battery is often still charged when it’s thrown away, and the casing hides it from sorting lines.

{{i:flame}} Fire

Batteries ignite when crushed

In a kerbside bin, a vape gets compacted in the truck and again at the facility. A punctured lithium cell can catch fire and spread to the whole load.

{{i:drop}} Chemicals

Nicotine and residues

Leftover e-liquid can contain nicotine and other chemicals. Dumped or landfilled vapes can leak them into soil and water, and they are harmful to children and pets.

{{i:cycle}} Lost resources

Materials worth recovering

Lithium-ion cells, copper wiring, steel and aluminium can all be recovered when vapes are collected separately and taken apart properly.

How the bin service works

Set up once. We handle the rest.

No storing boxes of vapes out the back, and no guessing what to do with them.

  1. Request a bin

    Tell us where it’ll go and roughly how many vapes you expect.

  2. We deliver it

    A lidded collection bin with signage showing what goes in, and what doesn’t.

  3. People fill it

    Customers, students or staff drop vapes in. Keep it out of direct sun with the lid closed.

  4. We collect & swap

    When it’s full, or on a set schedule, we take it away and leave an empty one.
