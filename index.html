Vape & e-cigarette recycling

Vapes don’t belong in any bin.

Every vape holds a lithium battery that can start a fire in a garbage truck or recycling plant. We supply collection bins to organisations, take drop-offs at our North Geelong facility, and make sure what’s collected is handled safely.

Why it has to be done properly

Batteries in the bin are setting the waste system on fire.

A “dead” vape usually isn’t. Its battery still holds charge, and when it’s crushed in a truck or a recycling plant, it can ignite the whole load.
